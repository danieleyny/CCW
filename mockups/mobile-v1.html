Separation comes from surface, not from lines

Today's questionnaire rows are a 1px rgba(255,255,255,.08) border on an 8px gap — at 390px that reads as one grey mass. Every list item becomes a card: a surface-2 → surface-1 gradient, an inset top highlight, a wide soft shadow, and a 12px gap. Same trick .card-raised already uses on the checklist.

State is felt before it's read

Unanswered = dashed signal ring. Answered No = the card recedes to 72% so your eye skips it. Answered Yes = brass left edge, brass glow, and the explanation textarea unfolds right there. You can tell where you are in thirteen questions without reading one.

The primary action never scrolls away

Step 4 is ~2,400px tall on a phone. Back/Next move into a sticky .glass-bar footer above the safe area, and the validation summary becomes a tap-to-jump pill docked to it — so "3 things to fix" is actionable instead of a list 2,000px from the field.

Nothing new in the palette

Every value here comes from paletteDark. What's new is rhythm: --tap:48px, --gutter:16px, --stack:12px, --stack-lg:20px, and a mobile type floor — body copy never below 13px, inputs at 16px so iOS never zooms.
