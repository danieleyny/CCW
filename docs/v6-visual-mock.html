What it takes

NYC gun license requirements

A NYC gun license comes down to four things: you must be at least 21, complete 18 hours of state-approved training, assemble about 24 documents, and file the application yourself with the NYPD License Division. None of it is hard on its own. All of it together is why people stall.

First: are you eligible?

Before you spend a dollar on training, find out whether you can apply at all.

  • You must be at least 21 years old to apply.
    Set by NYPD License Division · source · verified 2026-07-14
  • Sealed and dismissed arrests are still disclosed on a New York firearms application.
    Set by CPL Article 160 · source · verified 2026-07-14
  • Your training certificate must be dated within 6 months of when you file.
    Set by New York State (CCIA) · source · verified 2026-07-14
  • Four character references, each notarized, none of them relatives.
    Set by 38 RCNY §5-03(a)(1) · source · verified 2026-07-14
We can explain a rule. We can't tell you what your specific arrest means for your specific application — that's legal advice, and it takes a lawyer.
Lack of candor is a more common denial ground than the underlying conduct.
